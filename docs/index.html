Install and deploy

  1. Download and unzip the Mac release.
  2. Move NULLFRONT.app to your Applications folder.
  3. Open the app, choose a faction, and select Launch Skirmish.
A note about this early build

The app is not Developer ID signed or notarized. macOS may block the first launch. If you choose to open it, follow Apple's official Open Anyway guidance after attempting to open the app.
